Changes

Cancellation & Rescheduling FAQ

How to change plans without stress.

Open your booking in the dashboard and choose Reschedule. Pick a new slot from available times. Changes made well in advance are free.

Cancel at least 4 hours before your slot for a full refund to the original payment method. Late cancellations may incur a fee to compensate the reserved beautician.

You will receive live tracking updates. If delays become significant, support can help rebook or arrange an alternate professional.

Minor adjustments can often be handled with support. Larger service changes may require a new booking so pricing and duration stay accurate.
