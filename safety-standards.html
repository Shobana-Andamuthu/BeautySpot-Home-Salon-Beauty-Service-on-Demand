Trust & Care

Our Safety Standards

Every BeautySpot visit is built on verified professionals, hygienic tools, and products you can trust.

Home / Safety Standards
People First

Verified Professionals

We only send beauticians who pass identity checks, skill assessments, and home-service training.

  • Background verification before onboarding
  • Hands-on training for at-home service etiquette
  • Ongoing quality reviews from customer ratings
  • Visible profile, specialty, and rating before arrival
Clean Kit

Sanitized Tools

Every appointment starts with a sterilized kit and fresh disposables — no shared corners cut.

Pre-Visit Sterilization

Metal tools are sanitized before each home appointment.

Single-Use Disposables

Cotton, spatulas, and applicators are replaced for every client.

Sealed Travel Kits

Tools travel in dedicated cases to stay protected between visits.

On Arrival

Hygiene Protocol

A consistent checklist our beauticians follow in your home before any treatment begins.

Hand Hygiene

Hands are washed and sanitized thoroughly before kit setup, before touching your skin or hair, and between all treatment stages.

Protective Practices

Masks, disposable capes, and single-use gloves are used whenever the treatment type or customer preference requires them.

Clean Work Surface

A protective sanitized mat and organizer tray are laid out so products and tools never touch unprotected household surfaces.

Responsible Cleanup

Used disposables are immediately packed and removed in sealed bags so your space stays spotless and tidy after every visit.

Formulas You Trust

Premium Products

We choose reputable skincare, haircare, and makeup brands known for performance and skin-friendly ingredients.

  • Professional-grade facial and hair formulas
  • Options noted for sensitive skin when you share preferences
  • Fresh makeup and polish finishes for lasting wear
  • Product details available on request during your visit

Home Respect

Support Anytime

Secure Payments

Post-Visit Review

Your feedback directly shapes who stays on our verified roster.

Hygiene Certification

Every beautician undergoes periodic hygiene audits and safety refresher training.
