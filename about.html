Our Story

Born From A Simple Idea: Beauty Should Come To You.

BeautySpot began when we noticed how often women put off self-care — not for lack of desire, but for lack of time. Traffic, waiting rooms, and packed schedules made salon visits feel like another chore.

So we built a platform that brings trained beauticians, premium products, and hygienic tools straight to your door. From weekday glow facials to bridal mornings, every visit is designed to feel calm, personal, and professionally finished.

Today we serve homes across cities with verified experts who treat your living room like a private salon — quietly, carefully, and beautifully.

Mission

We exist to remove every barrier between women and the care they deserve — time, travel, and uncertainty.

  • Uphold hygiene and product standards on every single visit.
  • Create booking experiences that feel simple, transparent, and kind.
