  • Filter by occasion, duration, or treatment type

Browse live availability across morning, afternoon, and evening windows. Same-day slots open when beauticians are nearby — perfect for last-minute glow-ups before an event.

Your Visit Setup

Share Your Address & Get Matched

We bring the salon to you — then pair you with the right verified specialist.

Step 03

Enter Address

Add your home or venue details so your beautician arrives prepared with the right kit, products, and setup. Landmark notes and parking tips help them find you smoothly.

  • Optional gate or building instructions
  • Saved addresses for faster rebooking
Step 04

Beautician Assigned

Once your booking is confirmed, we assign a verified professional based on skill match, ratings, proximity, and availability — so you get the right expert for bridal glam, facials, hair, or nails.

  • Background-checked, trained home-service experts
  • Matched by specialty and appointment needs
  • Profile, rating, and specialty shared before arrival
